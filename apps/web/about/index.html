How it began

From a creative escape to something close to my heart.

What started as a simple love for beads slowly became something very personal. I have always loved creating little things with my hands—choosing colours, bringing different beads together and turning simple pieces into something beautiful.

Making bracelets became my creative escape: a place to enjoy the process and let my imagination lead. With time, I began to wonder, “Why not turn something I love into something I can share with others?” That is how HUSBA Beads came to life.

Every bracelet is handmade by me with care, from choosing the colours and beads to putting the final detail in place. I especially love creating combinations that feel personal to the person wearing them.

This is more than making bracelets—it is about creating little pieces of happiness that you can wear, gift or keep close. Thank you for supporting this small business. Every order, message, follow and kind word means more than you know.
