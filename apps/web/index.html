Founded in 2026 · Mumbai, India

What began as a simple love for beads became something deeply personal. Choosing colours, bringing different beads together and turning small details into beautiful pieces has always been a joyful creative escape.

HUSBA Beads was created to share that joy. Every bracelet is handmade with care—from the first colour choice to the final detail—with a special love for pieces that feel personal to the person wearing them.

Each creation is a little piece of happiness to wear, gift or keep close. Thank you for supporting this small business; every order, message and kind word means more than you know.
